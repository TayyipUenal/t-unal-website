### Audit & Zertifizierung → four-step quote configurator

### Motion system (revision 2, same day)
The mouse-following guide cursor from the first revision was removed again after review. In its place the page uses one small, consistent motion vocabulary (`src/components/motion.tsx`, `src/motion-tokens.ts`, `src/motion.css`), all of it short, one-shot and disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`:

- **Hero** enters with CSS keyframes (header fade, headline wipe line by line, lead, actions, film) so the prerendered page animates at first paint without waiting for JavaScript. Copy and film drift slightly at different rates while scrolling away.
- **Kicker**: a thin rule draws in, then the label follows. Used by every section.
- **Wipe**: section headings are revealed from behind a mask while sliding up.
- **Rise** and **Stagger**: paragraphs, cards, list items and the configurator card fade up a few pixels, lists one item after another.
- **Zusammenarbeit**: the step crossing the middle of the viewport is in focus while the others dim.
- Reveals are driven by a shared, rAF-throttled scroll measurement (`src/reveal.ts`, `useVisible` in `src/hooks.ts`) instead of an IntersectionObserver. An observer does not reliably report elements that are already on screen at load (deep link to `#angebot`, reload mid-page, restored scroll position), and a missed callback would leave a heading permanently invisible; measuring geometry makes the reveal deterministic.
- Training keeps its scroll-driven timeline; its stages now rise into view.

### Mail endpoint
`public/api/inquiry.php` now handles two kinds of payload: the existing consultation form (unchanged validation and messages) and `"kind": "quote"`. Both share origin, content-type, size, honeypot, header-injection and request-id checks, the hourly per-IP and global limits and the idempotent retry. `public/api/quote-template.php` validates the configurator payload against fixed allow-lists (mirrored in `src/quote.ts`) and renders the owner notification: subject `Angebotsanfrage: ISO 9001 + ISO 14001 [TU-…]`, a numbered "Auswahl im Konfigurator" block (steps 1–3, "zeitnah" badge for the 4-week option), "Schritt 4 · Kontaktdaten", optional note, reply button with pre-filled subject, and a complete plain-text alternative. The multipart assembly moved into `buildMultipartMessage()` in `inquiry-template.php` and is shared by both templates. Sender stays `info@tayyipuenal.de`; Reply-To is the visitor.

The privacy page now names the configurator data (Norm, Ausgangslage, Unternehmensgröße, Zeitrahmen, Anmerkung). The home meta description mentions the four-step offer.
